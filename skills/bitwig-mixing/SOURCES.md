increments and the diagnostic sequence are labelled local editorial defaults.

automatic EQ/compression and treating native settings as acoustic proof.
Deferred: standardized perceptual masking scores and automatic patch calibration.

Author walkthroughs: an authorized balance pass reads/renders/edits/verifies;
a spectral-overlap-only complaint requires a contextual test; advice-only requests
do not touch the host; a mastering-only request is outside this skill. These are
author inspections, not fresh-context agent experiments.
